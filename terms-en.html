FlashCard Purchase, Subscription and Restore Terms

Last updated: October 24, 2026. FlashCard is being prepared for release and is not currently available on the App Store. Available plans, prices and service scope follow the released app and Apple purchase confirmation.

Free use and Pro

Ordinary photo collection, access to originals, viewing and re-exporting saved works, complete backup and restore remain free. The free tier allows three successfully saved new advanced flashcards per device per UTC calendar month. Each device maintains its own allowance. Failed or cancelled saves, subsequent edits to an existing work and repeated exports do not use another allowance. Pro offers unlimited new works through monthly or annual subscriptions or a one-time purchase.

Subscriptions, trial and renewal

Monthly and annual subscriptions renew automatically. A seven-day annual trial is offered only when Apple reports both an available offer and account eligibility. The purchase page and Apple confirmation show the actual trial, full renewal price and billing period. After a trial, the subscription renews at the displayed annual price unless cancelled. Manage or cancel it in Apple subscription settings at least 24 hours before renewal.

Apple processes payment. The app does not request payment-card details for the developer. Available plans and prices follow the customer's storefront and Apple purchase confirmation. Internal regional target prices are not a promise of an available storefront price.

Lifetime purchase and restore

The lifetime option is a one-time non-consumable purchase. It does not automatically cancel an existing subscription. Customers who no longer need that subscription must cancel it separately in Apple subscription settings. Restore Purchases retrieves eligible access for the same Apple purchase account without initiating a new charge. Refunds, account eligibility and any configured sharing follow Apple's rules; unconfigured Family Sharing is not promised.
