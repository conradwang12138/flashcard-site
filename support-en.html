FlashCard Support

Last updated: October 24, 2026. FlashCard is being prepared for release and is not currently available on the App Store. Owner: Ziheng Wang. Contact: wzh925704928@gmail.com.

Collect first, or create one FlashCard

Choose “收纳” to select several photos or take a series of photographs. Originals are saved first and shared details can be skipped. Choose “做闪卡” to start with one photo and preview its outline and material. Editing an existing item's photograph adds an appearance; it does not add to the physical quantity.

Correct an outline or selection

Automatic suggestions may miss a faint border, reflection, occlusion, hole or base. Correct the object's outline first. Adjust the foreground with the brush or eraser when depth is needed. If there is no usable foreground selection, choose the whole-card effect. Original photos remain available.

Save and export

The app provides a local “闪卡／导出” folder in Files. If the picker starts at On My iPhone instead, open the FlashCard folder and then “导出”, or choose another writable destination. The image is saved only after the system confirms the copy. Generating a preview or cancelling the picker does not mean the file has been saved.

Saving a work keeps editable data in the library. Generating a file is separate from saving it to Photos, saving it to Files or sharing it. MP4, Live Photo and GIF outputs play a fixed animation; live tilt interaction stays in the app. Receiving platforms may convert or compress the media.

Make a showcase page

In the collection’s ellipsis menu, choose “选择多条”, select 1–9 items, then “展示页”. Choose a grid, one hero item or a shelf layout and a 1:1, 4:5 or 9:16 canvas. The first item is the hero; use the order controls to change it. Names are hidden by default. Generate a JPEG to share or save to Files. This static presentation is not an editable backup. Object recognition provides suggestions, not a guarantee of complete borders or holes; keep the original and correct the outline manually when needed.

Back up and restore

A portable data ZIP contains common images and records. A complete backup also keeps originals, masks and effect recipes. Restore shows a preview before applying changes. The current limits are 200,000,000 bytes per file, 2,000,000,000 expanded bytes per archive, 30,000 file/directory entries, and 20,000 items in a portable migration. Unsupported archives are rejected. Split-volume backup is not currently available.

Previous cloud data

Do not remove the only cloud or local copy when migrating. Verify that originals, appearances and pending content are present, then restore a complete backup into an empty library. Preserve the previous installation while content is missing or unresolved.

Purchases and subscriptions

Ordinary collection, original-photo access, viewing and re-exporting saved works, complete backup/restore remain free. The free plan allows three successfully saved new advanced works per device per UTC calendar month; failed/cancelled saves, edits to the same work, and re-exports do not count again. Pro offers unlimited new works through monthly/annual subscriptions or a one-time lifetime purchase. Actual available plans and prices follow the released app and Apple purchase confirmation. The annual seven-day trial appears only when StoreKit returns both eligibility and an available offer. Subscription prices and renewal terms must match the purchase confirmation sheet. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple Account subscription settings. A lifetime purchase does not cancel an existing subscription. The app includes Restore Purchases, Manage Subscriptions, Terms and Privacy entry points.
