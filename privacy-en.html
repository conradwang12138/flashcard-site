FlashCard Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026. Owner: Ziheng Wang. Support: wzh925704928@gmail.com.

FlashCard stores selected photographs, collection details, object masks and effect recipes on your device. You may record quantities, costs, storage locations and notes. Image analysis uses on-device Apple frameworks. The inspected app has no developer-operated media server, external AI service or advertising/tracking SDK.

Optional iCloud sync uses Apple's CloudKit private database for your Apple Account, subject to account, network and storage availability. Turning sync off retains local items; synchronization is not an independent versioned backup and deletions may propagate.

Camera, photo and motion access support the features you choose. Exports may include original-photo metadata, prices, locations and notes. Review them before sharing. Copies sent to other applications or cloud drives are governed by those destinations. ZIPFoundation is used locally for archives. Apple diagnostics follow your settings and Apple's policies; this policy covers the app and the support information you choose to send.

Showcase images: selected collection photos or appearances can be composed into a new JPEG. Item names are hidden by default and can be enabled by you. The image does not attach collection prices, storage locations, notes or original-photo location metadata. Photographed content, your title and names you choose to show may still contain private information. Check the preview before sharing. A showcase image is not an editable backup.

Purchases and subscriptions: the app reads product and entitlement information through Apple StoreKit, including an entitlement check when the app starts. You initiate purchases and restoration; Apple processes payment. The app does not ask you to provide payment-card details to the developer. The inspected source has no path that sends transaction receipts or purchase history to a developer server. Pro access requires a StoreKit-verified result. The free creation allowance is recorded in the local database per device per UTC calendar month and is excluded from collection cloud sync and backup packages. Apple handles App Store purchase information under its policies. Prices follow the purchase confirmation sheet. A trial is shown only when StoreKit returns both eligibility and an available offer. See the Purchase and Subscription Terms.

Voluntary support: the developer receives the email address, problem description and attachments you choose to send. The app's problem-report template contains only the app version, system version and blank steps; it does not automatically attach originals, a collection library or diagnostic logs. Remove private information and never send passwords, verification codes or payment details. Support email is used only to answer and resolve your issue, not for marketing. Support messages and attachments are deleted90days after resolution, unless retention is required by law or a dispute. You may contact the address above to request access, correction or deletion of the support information you chose to send.

Copies you choose to save to Files can be browsed, shared or deleted through the system file interface. The app provides a local FlashCard export folder. The collection database, original photos, masks and recipes remain in private application storage; providing an export folder does not move them into it.

Manage local items, recent deletions, sync and exports in the app. Uninstalling does not necessarily delete iCloud or exported copies. Keep a verified backup first.
